# Monster Reborn / 死者苏生

## monster-reborn-origin

### English

Monster Reborn targets one monster in either player's Graveyard and Special Summons it. The source of that summon is the Graveyard.

### 简体中文

「死者苏生」以任一方墓地里的一只怪兽为对象，将其特殊召唤。这次召唤的来源是墓地。
